# Writing quality bar

What the first draft must meet. The review loop is in `SKILL.md`, the
reviewer prompts in `reviewer-prompts.md`, the figure and table conventions in
`figure-map-schema.md`, the voice in `writing-style.md`.

- **A narrative, not a transcript.** Read the whole notes file first, decide
  the story (what problem, what key idea, why a reader should care), and
  write toward it instead of replaying the notes section by section.
- **Concise 前言 and 結論.** `## 前言` orients the reader in a couple of short
  paragraphs and says what the article covers; `## 結論` quickly recaps the
  takeaways. Neither runs as long as a body section.
- **Completeness over brevity: long is fine, thin is not.** Carry forward
  everything substantive in the notes, including asides flagged as valuable
  even when tangential. Cutting means removing a restated point or a
  content-free transition, not depth.
- **Explain from first principles.** Plain language before symbols and terms
  of art. Prioritize what is practically useful to an engineer.
- **Honest, not promotional.** The notes' limitations, caveats and open
  questions go in, usually as one distinct "where this could bite you"
  section near the end. Keep the notes' certainty: an inference stays an
  inference, a "may" stays a "may".
- **Paraphrase and attribute.** Don't reproduce the source verbatim.
- **Every claim traces to the notes.** Don't fill gaps from general knowledge
  or guess. A made-up illustrative example is fine when marked as one.
- **A visual where it supports the text.** Place each figure or table at the
  paragraph it supports, not batched at the end; only the ones the notes
  mention (`SKILL.md`, rule 6).
